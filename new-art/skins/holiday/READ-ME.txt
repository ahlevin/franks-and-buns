FRANKS & BUNS HOLIDAY SKINS

Eight source PNG strips created with the built-in image generator.
Each strip contains three states, left to right: frank alone, plain bun, grilled bun with steam.
Trim into individual sprites and check scale/body anchors in the actual game before shipping. These images are not yet integrated. Costume details should not expand collision boxes. No animation pairs are included in this collection.
