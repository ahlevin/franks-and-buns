FRANKS & BUNS — Final Boss Franks

5 costume skins, created with the built-in image generator.
Each transparent PNG is 2172 x 724 pixels and contains three states:
LEFT: costumed frank alone.
CENTER: same costume in a plain golden bun.
RIGHT: same costume in a grilled bun with steam.

Included skins:
- armored-knight
- frank-exe
- lightning-fighter
- ninja
- shadow-wizard

PREPARING FOR THE GAME
These are source art strips, not yet integrated or tested in gameplay.
Separate the three states, trim transparent padding and align the sausage
body anchor and scale to the existing game sprites. Inspect each crop rather
than assuming identical visual bounds. Keep costume decorations out of
collision calculations. Test readability at actual mobile display size.
Steam, sparkles, lightning and other effects are baked into the images;
independent animation needs separate layers. No animation pose pairs included.
Exact generation prompts are included in prompts.txt.
