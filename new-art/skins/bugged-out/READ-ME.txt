PREPARING FOR THE GAME
These source strips have not been integrated or tested in gameplay.
Separate the three states and trim transparent padding. Align the sausage
body anchor and scale to your existing sprites. Inspect each crop's visual
bounds; do not assume identical dimensions. Ignore near-transparent pixels
when finding trim bounds (Professor Frank and Special Delivery include
corner pixels with alpha 1/255). Keep costume decorations outside collision
calculations. Test readability at mobile size, especially report-card grades.
Effects, wings, steam and accessories are baked into the artwork. Independent
animation requires separate layers or additional poses. No animation pairs
are included. The firefly light is static, not an animated glow.

Exact generation prompts are included in prompts.txt.
