PREPARATION
Source art has not been integrated or tested in gameplay.
Separate the three sprites, trim padding, and align the sausage body anchor
and scale to your existing game art. Inspect visual bounds of each crop.
Ignore near-transparent pixels when calculating automatic trim bounds;
Touchdown and Royal Treatment contain corner pixels with alpha 1/255.
Keep decorations outside collision calculations. Check readability at mobile
size. Steam, accessories and highlights are baked in; independent motion
requires separate layers. No alternate animation poses are included.

Exact prompts are included in prompts.txt.
