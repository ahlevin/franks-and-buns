# More City Kitchens: Chicago, Coney Island, Ballpark

Same recipe and color rules as New York and San Francisco (see city-kitchens-art-prompts.md). Attach **chef.png**, **dog.png** and **bg-wall-a.jpg** as style references, plus the finished **nyc-back-wall** so every city matches.

## Tips learned from New York and San Francisco
- **Edges**: the wall and counter pictures scroll and repeat. The game can hide a mismatch by flipping every other copy, but matching left and right edges looks best. Each wall and counter prompt asks for this.
- **Train, car or boat**: the generator can't make real transparency and draws a fake checkerboard instead, so each passer-by prompt asks for a plain solid white background. I cut it out on my end.
- **Sizes**: any size close to the ones asked for is fine.
