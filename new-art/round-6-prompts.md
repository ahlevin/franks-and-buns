# Kitchens, round 6: Washington D.C., Hong Kong, Seoul, Swiss Alps, Prague

These use the same recipe and color rules as the other cities. Attach **chef.png**, **dog.png** and the finished **nyc-back-wall** as references for every picture. Each city needs 4 pictures: back wall, counter, start screen and passer-by.

Notes:
- **Landmarks**: these are drawn as original cartoon shapes, not exact copies (the Capitol, the Washington Monument, Hong Kong's towers, N Seoul Tower, the Matterhorn, Prague Castle and Charles Bridge), with no names or lettering. Real colors that would fight the buns (red Swiss and Prague trains, Prague's orange roofs, Hong Kong's green trams) are drawn cool in the wall and passer, with full color on the start screens.
- **Passer-by**: each one asks for a plain white background, which I cut out.
- **Colors**: cool and vivid in the middle of the picture, warm only in the top sunset band and small lights, and full drama on the start screen.
