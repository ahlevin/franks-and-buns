# Christmas Edition: art prompts

**How the mode works:** the frank becomes a **wrapped present**. The player drops it into **Santa's sleigh**, which scrolls in like a bun. After 6 loads, a bonus target appears in the sleigh's place: **Santa holding his open gift bag**, worth more, like the grilled bun today.

**Character swaps:**
- **Mrs. Claus** takes the chef's place. She chases the dog in the intro, and her arm pops up between sleighs holding a big candy cane instead of the fork.
- **The dog** wears a reindeer-antler headband.
- **Rudolph** (glowing red nose) replaces the chili pepper for the bonus rush.
- **Elves** cheer when a sleigh load is done and ride a toy train as the passer-by.
- **Christmas treats** replace the condiments.

These follow the same system as the sports editions (soccer, baseball and the others), so every picture has a matching slot in the game.

**References:** attach **chef.png** and **dog.png** as STYLE references to every prompt. For the backgrounds, also attach **bg-wall-nyc.jpg** for line weight and palette rules. Sprites ask for a plain solid **pure white background**, which I cut out.

**Size targets I'll scale to:** sleigh 352×148, present 328×112, treats 64×128, Rudolph 320×255 (2 frames), dog 280×208 (2 frames), Mrs. Claus 512×512, her arm 512×768, back wall 3:2, counter 3:1, start screen 9:16.

**Color rule:** sleighs, presents and Santa are the warm colors the player tracks: red, gold and green. So the backgrounds stay **cool** (night blues, violets, teals, snow in cool lavender-white), with warm color only in tiny window lights.

**Keep it original:** no real logos, brand mascots, movie characters, words or numbers.

## 1. christmas-back-wall
Use case: stylized-concept. Generate one finished game background asset, not a sheet or mockup.
Input images are STYLE REFERENCES ONLY: chef and dog. Match their bold dark outlines, appealing rounded cartoon shapes and crisp cel shading. Do not include these characters. Match the line style and palette approach of the New York back wall reference.
Asset: BACK WALL for the Christmas Edition of Franks and Buns, a 2D side-scrolling arcade game. Landscape 3:2, request 3072 x 2048 or the largest available.
The North Pole at night, seen straight on. Upper part: a deep indigo night sky with soft swirling northern lights in teal, cyan and violet, a scatter of small twinkling stars, and a big pale full moon toward one side. Along the skyline: Santa's workshop village drawn as original cartoon shapes: chunky timber cottages and a tall workshop with steep snow-covered roofs, icicles on the eaves, smoking chimneys, round windows glowing with tiny warm lights, and frosted pine trees in cool blue-green. Snowflakes drift gently. A smooth snowy hill crosses at about 62% height. Lower part: a cool lavender-white snowbank with a simple low candy-striped fence in pale periwinkle and white, low contrast.
Palette rules: the middle band (15% to 85% of the height) stays mid-value, cool and calm: blues, violets, teals, lavender, cool snow. No large red, orange, tan, gold, yellow, bright green or pure white areas there. Warm color only as tiny window lights.
Perfectly flat orthographic straight-on elevation, no vanishing point. Seamless horizontal tiling: the left and right edges must match exactly so copies can sit side by side; keep the big workshop and moon away from both side edges. No vignette.
No people, Santa, elves, reindeer, animals, food, hot dogs, buns, presents, sleighs, vehicles, signs, shelves, countertops, platforms, text, letters, numbers, logos, UI or watermark. Opaque full-bleed background.
