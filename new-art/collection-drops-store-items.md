# Store items for the weekly Collection drops

Each Collection goes on sale by itself at midnight Pacific on its drop date. Its store items have to exist by then in all three places:

1. **Stripe** (website). Add each one to RevenueCat's **web_shop** offering as a package with the same id. Then send me the Stripe price and product ids (`price_…`, `prod_…`), so the website recognizes the purchases.
2. **App Store Connect** (in-app purchase, non-consumable).
3. **Google Play** (in-app product).

Use exactly the same product ids, prices and setup as Sports and Deep Sea Dogs:
- $0.99 for each costume.
- $3.99 for the Collection, or $2.99 at the launch price through Jan 15.

If a drop's items aren't ready on time, the Collection still appears and BunBux still work, but its Buy buttons say "Not available yet". Tell me and I can move that drop's date instead.

★ marks a Limited Edition costume: purchase only, never BunBux.

Total: 18 Collections, 87 costumes, so 105 items in each store.
