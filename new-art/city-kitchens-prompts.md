# City Kitchens: art prompts

These are for the image generator in your other project. They follow the same recipe as the current kitchen art (new-art/README.md in the website repo). Give it chef.png and dog.png as style references, plus bg-wall-a.jpg as the color and line reference.

Each city needs 4 pictures:
1. **Back wall**: landscape 3:2, scrolls slowly behind the game and repeats left to right
2. **Counter strip**: very wide 3:1, the counter and ground at the bottom of the screen, repeats left to right
3. **Start screen**: portrait 9:16, with a calm empty middle for the title and buttons
4. **Passer-by**: a transparent picture of a train, cable car or boat that rolls across the background now and then

Rules for every picture: stylized cartoon landmarks only. No real logos, transit maps, route numbers, team names, store names or readable words anywhere, including the graffiti. No people, animals, food or hot dogs (the game draws those).

## New York City

### nyc-back-wall
Use case: stylized-concept. Generate one finished game background asset, not a sheet or mockup.
Input images are STYLE REFERENCES ONLY: chef and dog. Match their bold dark outlines, appealing rounded cartoon shapes and crisp cel shading, while keeping the background lower contrast and more muted. Do not include these characters or copy their gray backdrops.
Asset: BACK WALL for the New York City level of Franks and Buns, a 2D side-scrolling arcade game. Landscape 3:2, request 3072 x 2048 pixels or largest available landscape resolution.
A sunny New York street scene seen straight on: in the upper half, a distant stylized Manhattan skyline with an Art Deco spire tower and a stepped skyscraper, and a stylized stone suspension bridge with Gothic arches and draped cables crossing the sky on one side. In the lower half, a red-brick wall with a few colorful abstract graffiti shapes (bubbly blobs, stars, swooshes, drips, NO letters or words), a black iron fire escape on one building, and a green street lamp. Keep props sparse in the upper half and generous quiet brick space below for game objects.
Perfectly flat orthographic straight-on elevation, no perspective vanishing point. Even lighting, no vignette. Seamless horizontal repetition: left and right edges must match precisely; keep landmarks away from both side edges. Soft flat shading, muted brick red, sky blue, steel grey and warm gold.
No people, animals, food, hot dogs, buns, vehicles, shelves, countertops, platforms, floor, text, letters, numbers, logos, signs, UI or watermark. Opaque full-bleed background.

### nyc-counter
Use case: stylized-concept. Generate one finished COUNTER AND GROUND STRIP for a 2D side-scrolling mobile arcade game, not a scene mockup. Chef and dog are STYLE references only, no characters; the NYC back wall is the palette reference. Very wide 3:1, target 3264x1088 or largest wide format available. Full bleed opaque.
A long shiny stainless-steel street-cart counter EDGE flush against the very TOP of the image (top 8 percent), then brushed steel cart panels with simple rivets and a bright yellow-and-blue striped umbrella-colored trim band (roughly next 60 percent), then a grey city sidewalk of square concrete slabs with a curb along the bottom 30 percent. No wall or sky above the counter. Flat straight-on elevation, no vanishing point, identical slab sizes, no converging lines. Bold clean cartoon outlines and soft flat cel shading. Exactly seamless horizontal repetition with an integer number of repeating panels. No people, animals, food, objects on the counter, text, logos, numbers, UI or watermark.
