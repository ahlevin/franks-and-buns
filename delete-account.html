Delete Your Packs Profile

Franks and Buns (Franks & Buns) by SLAE Labs LLC · Last updated: November 19, 2026

Franks and Buns doesn't need an account to play. The only account-like data we keep is your optional Packs profile: the nickname you chose, a random player ID, your Packs scores and which Packs you are in. You can delete it at any time.

Delete it in the game (instant)

  1. Open Franks and Buns (the iPhone, iPad or Android app, or franksandbuns.com/game.html) on the device where you use Packs.
  2. On the start screen, tap MY PACKS.
  3. Tap Recovery code at the bottom of the screen.
  4. Tap Delete my Packs profile, then tap it again to confirm.

Your profile is deleted from our servers right away.

Or ask us by email

If you can't use the game (for example, you lost your phone), email admin@slaelabs.com with the subject “Delete my Packs profile”. Include your Packs nickname and the names or codes of your Packs so we can find your profile. We will confirm and delete it within 30 days. Never send us your recovery code.

What is deleted

  • Your nickname and player ID
  • All of your Packs scores (weekly and all-time)
  • Your Pack memberships and any requests to join a Pack
  • Your recovery code stops working

What happens to your Packs

If you created a Pack, it passes to the player who has been in it longest. If you were the only player, the Pack is deleted.

What is kept

  • Nothing from your Packs profile is kept after deletion.
  • To limit abuse, we keep a one-way scrambled form of IP addresses for one day; these records are deleted automatically.
  • Scores saved only on your device (your best score and your own Wienerboard) are not affected; they stay on your device until you clear the browser's site data or delete the app.

See our Privacy Policy for everything Franks and Buns collects. Questions: admin@slaelabs.com.
