Franks & Buns: The Complete Guide 🌭

In one sentence: Franks & Buns is a free one-tap arcade game where you fly a runaway hot dog into scrolling buns, dodge the chef's fork, and chase high scores, playable in any web browser at franksandbuns.com and as an app for iPhone, iPad and Android.

What is Franks & Buns?

Franks & Buns is a casual arcade game about a hot dog on the run. A sneaky dog steals a hot dog, the chef gives chase, and when the chef trips over the pup the frank goes flying. That airborne hot dog is you. You tap to keep it in the air and time your drops so it lands in buns as they scroll past.

Each run lasts a few minutes, uses a single control (tap, click or press Space), and gets faster the longer you survive. The game is free to play with no account needed. It is made by SLAE Labs LLC, a small independent studio in California.

How do you play Franks & Buns?

Hold or tap to boost your frank upward and let go to let gravity pull it down. Buns scroll from right to left on counters, shelves, ovens and fridges at different heights. Land the frank in a bun to score. Missing a bun, hitting the floor, or touching the chef's fork costs a life. You start with three lives.

  • Boxes: every 6 franks you land fills a box, earns a bonus and speeds up the kitchen.
  • The chef's fork: it shoots up between buns without warning. Dodge it.
  • The dog: after box 3 the dog shows up in play and is worth 1,000 points.
  • Dog Parade: every 4 boxes, dogs stream across the screen for 5 seconds, worth 500 points each, and you earn an extra life afterward.
  • Chili Rush: catch a floating chili dog and your frank is red-hot for 6 seconds, scoring double and shielded from losing a life.
  • Grilled buns: after box 6, landing in a grilled bun gives a flat 1,000-point bonus.
  • Stages: after each Dog Parade your run moves up a stage, with its own name and a bonus of 1,000 points for every stage past the first.
  • Second Wind: once per run, when your last life is gone, you can spend 50 BunBux to keep playing.

What are Game Modes?

Tap the GAME MODE button on the start screen or the Game Over screen to choose how you play. The mode you pick stays on, even after you close the game, until you choose another one. Every mode uses the same one-tap controls, scoring, BunBux and Wienerboards.

  • Classic: the original game. Fly the hot dog into the buns.
  • Soccer: drop the ball into the goals in a night stadium. Pop-up: GOAL!
  • Basketball: drop the ball through the hoops in a packed arena. Pop-up: SWISH!
  • Football: drop the ball into a receiver's gloved hands on the gridiron. Pop-up: CAUGHT IT!
  • Water Polo: drop the ball into floating goals at the pool. Pop-up: GOAL!
  • Golf: drop the golf ball into the cups on floating greens. Pop-up: IN THE CUP!
  • Baseball: drop the ball into catcher's mitts at the ballpark. Pop-up: STRIKE!
  • Cricket: knock the bails off the stumps on a village green. Pop-up: HOWZAT!
  • Ice Hockey: slap the puck into the nets at the rink. Pop-up: LIGHT THE LAMP!

In each sport a player in a pinstriped number 25 home kit runs along under the ball, a rival tries to block it, the chili dog becomes a fire ball, and the stages have their own names. Sports modes always play in their own venue. Your costumes and kitchens come back when you switch to Classic.

How does scoring work?

Every landed frank is worth 100 base points. Condiment buns multiply that: mustard 2x, ketchup 3x, relish 5x, cheese 5x and jalapeño 10x. Streaks stack on top: 3 landings in a row is On A Roll (1.5x), 5 in a row is Franks On Fire (2x), and 10 in a row is Wiener Wizard (3x). One miss resets the streak.

Your Wienerboard keeps your top 5 runs on the device you play on. For more detail, see How to Play & Scoring.

What are costumes and Collections?

Costumes (also called skins) change how your hot dog looks in flight, in the bun and on the grill. They are cosmetic only and never change scoring or difficulty.

  • Free and earned costumes: a few are free from the start, and others unlock by hitting in-game goals, such as landing 10 in a row or reaching the Dog Parade.
  • Collections: themed sets of 4 to 11 costumes, such as Holiday, Dogs, Cats, Sports and Deep Sea Dogs. New Collections are released on Tuesdays.
  • Buying: costumes and whole Collections can be bought in the game. In the US a single costume is typically $0.99. Prices vary by store and region.
  • Limited Edition: one or two costumes in most Collections are Limited Edition and can only be bought, not unlocked with BunBux.

What are BunBux?

BunBux are the in-game currency of Franks & Buns. They are earned by playing and cannot be bought with money.

  • 1 BunBux for each box you pack, up to 10 per game.
  • 25 BunBux for completing the Daily Challenge, plus bonuses for playing on consecutive days (a day streak).
  • You can earn up to 100 BunBux per day.
  • Spend 300 BunBux to unlock most non-Limited costumes, or 500 BunBux to unlock a kitchen.

What are kitchens?

Kitchens are the backgrounds you play in. Besides the Classic Kitchen there are around 50 themed kitchens grouped into US Cities, Europe, Latin America, Asia & Middle East, Amusement and Campus towns. Examples include New York, San Francisco, Coney Island, Paris, Rome, Rio de Janeiro, Hong Kong, a Ballpark and a Golf Course. Some kitchens unlock by reaching a goal (for example, packing 6 boxes in one game), and the rest unlock for 500 BunBux. Kitchens are cosmetic and do not change the rules.

Can you play with friends?

Yes. A Dog House is a private leaderboard for friends, family or coworkers. Create one with no email or password, then share a link or a 6-character code. Each Dog House has a This Week board (resets Monday at midnight UTC) and an All-Time board. Every game you play counts in all of your Dog Houses. You can join up to 5 Dog Houses, each with up to 50 players. Details are on the About page.

Frequently asked questions

Is Franks & Buns free?

Yes. The whole game is free in the browser and in the apps. Optional purchases are cosmetic costumes only.

Do I need an account?

No. You can play right away. Dog Houses use a recovery code instead of an email or password.

Is it pay to win?

No. Costumes and kitchens are cosmetic and never change scoring, speed or lives.

Can I buy BunBux?

No. BunBux are earned only by playing: packing boxes, finishing the Daily Challenge and keeping a day streak.

How often do new costumes come out?

New themed Collections are released on Tuesdays.

Can I play sports instead of hot dogs?

Yes. Tap GAME MODE on the start screen or after a game and pick Soccer, Basketball, Football, Water Polo, Golf, Baseball, Cricket or Ice Hockey. Your pick stays on until you change it.

Does it work offline?

Yes, the core game can run offline on your device. Features like BunBux, purchases and Dog Houses would require an internet connection.

Who makes Franks & Buns?

SLAE Labs LLC, an independent studio in California. Contact admin@slaelabs.com.
