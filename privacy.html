Privacy Policy

Last updated: September 30, 2026

This Privacy Policy explains what information Franks & Buns collects, how it is used and shared, and the choices you have. It covers the website at franksandbuns.com (the "Site") and the Franks & Buns app for iPhone (the "App"). Franks & Buns is operated by SLAE Labs LLC, a California limited liability company ("we", "us", or "our").

1. Information We Collect

You don't need an account to play, and the game never asks for your name, email address, or phone number. We collect:

  • Usage data — pages and screens viewed, time spent, game events, device and browser type, and approximate location (city or region, derived from your IP address), collected by Google Analytics on the Site and in the App. Google Analytics assigns your browser or device a random identifier; it does not identify you by name.
  • Wienerboard entries — if you choose to post a top score, we store the nickname you type, your score, your run statistics (franks landed, boxes completed, best streak), and the date. Posting is optional.
  • Your best score — saved on your own device (browser local storage) so the game can show it. It is not sent to us unless you post it to the Wienerboard.
  • Advertising data (Site only) — on the Site, Google AdSense may use cookies and your IP address to show ads. The App contains no ads.

2. How We Use Information

  • To run the game and the Wienerboard leaderboard
  • To understand how the game is played and improve it (Google Analytics)
  • To show ads on the Site (Google AdSense)

We do not sell your information, and the App does not track you across other companies' apps or websites.

3. The Wienerboard Leaderboard

Nicknames and scores posted to the Wienerboard are public: anyone playing on the Site or in the App can see the top scores. Please don't use your real name or any personal information as a nickname. Nicknames are checked for offensive words, and we remove entries that are offensive or impersonate someone.

To report a nickname, tap it on the Wienerboard and choose Report this name, or email askfranksandbuns@gmail.com. We review reports within 48 hours and remove nicknames that break our rules. Choose Hide this player to stop seeing a name on your device. To have your own entry removed, email us with the nickname, score, and approximate date.

4. Service Providers

We share information only with the providers that run parts of the game, and only for the purposes above. We choose providers that protect data to the same or a higher standard than this policy, and each processes it under its own privacy terms:

  • Google Analytics (Google LLC) — usage measurement on the Site and in the App. Google Privacy Policy
  • Google AdSense (Google LLC) — advertising on the Site only. See how Google uses data when you use our partners' sites or apps.
  • Supabase (Supabase, Inc.) — hosts the Wienerboard database. Supabase Privacy Policy

5. Cookies and Local Storage

  • Analytics cookies — Google Analytics, on the Site and in the App.
  • Advertising cookies — Google AdSense, on the Site only.
  • Local storage — your best score, kept on your device.

Where required, visitors to the Site from the EEA, UK, and Switzerland are asked for consent before advertising cookies are set. You can clear cookies and local storage in your browser settings, and deleting the App removes the data it stores on your device. You can opt out of personalized ads at Google Ad Settings or aboutads.info, and out of Google Analytics on the Site with Google's opt-out browser add-on.

6. How Long We Keep Information

  • Wienerboard entries — until removed, on your request or when we reset the board.
  • Google Analytics data — no longer than 14 months, then deleted automatically.
  • Your best score — on your device until you clear it or delete the App.

7. Your Choices and Rights

You can ask us to remove a Wienerboard entry at any time, and you can stop analytics and personalized ads using the opt-outs above. Because we don't collect names or email addresses, we identify Wienerboard entries by nickname, score, and date.

If you are in the European Economic Area, United Kingdom, or Switzerland, you have the right to access, correct, delete, restrict, or object to the processing of your personal data, to data portability, to withdraw consent at any time, and to complain to your local data protection authority. For advertising data held by Google, please contact Google directly.

California: we do not sell personal information. On the Site, Google AdSense may use cookies for personalized advertising, which California law may treat as "sharing"; you can opt out at Google Ad Settings. The Site does not respond to browser "Do Not Track" signals; use the opt-outs above instead.

8. Children's Privacy

Franks & Buns is a general-audience game and is not directed at children under 13. We do not knowingly collect personal information from children under 13. The game never asks for a name, email address, or contact details. If you believe a child has posted personal information as a Wienerboard nickname, email us and we will delete it.

9. Security

Information sent between the game and our service providers is encrypted in transit (HTTPS). The Wienerboard stores no names, email addresses, or contact details.

10. Third-Party Links

The Site and App may link to third-party websites. We are not responsible for the privacy practices of those sites and encourage you to review their privacy policies.

11. Changes to This Policy

We may update this Privacy Policy from time to time. Changes will be posted on this page with an updated date. Continued use of the Site or App after changes constitutes your acceptance of the updated policy.
