Contact Franks & Buns

Who makes Franks & Buns

Franks & Buns is made and published by SLAE Labs LLC, a small independent game studio and California limited liability company in the United States. We run the website franksandbuns.com and the Franks & Buns apps for iPhone, iPad and Android.

How to reach us

Email admin@slaelabs.com for anything about the game. We usually reply within a few days. Please include what you were doing, which device you play on (web, iPhone, iPad or Android), and a screenshot if it helps.

  • Game help and bugs: what happened and on which device.
  • Purchases: app purchases are handled by Apple or Google, and refunds are requested through them. For website purchases, email us with the date and the costume.
  • Dog House reports: use Report a name or Dog House in the game, or email us the Dog House code.
  • Privacy and data deletion: see the Privacy Policy and how to delete your data.
  • Press, creators and partnerships: put "Press" in the subject. Facts and links are in the Complete Guide.

Reporting a security issue

If you think you have found a security or privacy problem in the website, the apps or our servers, please email admin@slaelabs.com with "Security" in the subject. Tell us what you found and how to reproduce it.

  • We will confirm we received your report and keep you updated while we fix it.
  • Please give us a reasonable time to fix the issue before sharing it publicly.
  • Please do not access other players' data, disrupt the game for others, or run automated attacks or load tests.

Our machine-readable contact is at /.well-known/security.txt.
